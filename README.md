## about

Moving across borders as a trans person is daunting. When I was navigating my own relocation to Iceland, I found plenty of sites tracking *what the law says* — but almost nothing on how to actually **execute the move**.

This space is my answer to that gap: turning abstract legal rights into structured, realistic survival data for the trans community.
