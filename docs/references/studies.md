# Peer-Reviewed Studies

## Key Studies on Trans Feminine HRT

| Study | Year | Key Findings |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **de Blok CJM, et al.** — *Breast cancer risk in transgender people receiving hormone treatment* (BMJ) | 2019 | Trans women have ~47× higher breast cancer risk than cis men, but ~3× lower than cis women |
| **Nota NM, et al.** — *Occurrence of acute cardiovascular events* (Circulation) | 2019 | Low absolute risk of cardiovascular events in trans women on HRT |
| **Connelly PJ, et al.** — *Managing VTE risk in transgender adults* | 2021 | Transdermal estradiol has lower VTE risk than oral |
| **Weill A, et al.** — *CPA and meningioma risk* (BMJ) | 2021 | Dose-dependent meningioma risk with CPA |

## Key Studies on Trans Masculine HRT

| Study | Year | Key Findings |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **van Velzen DM, et al.** — *Long-term effects of T therapy* | 2021 | T therapy is safe with appropriate monitoring |
| **Gava G, et al.** — *Metabolic effects of T therapy* | 2020 | Minimal adverse metabolic effects in healthy individuals |
