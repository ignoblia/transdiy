# Glossary of Terms

## A

**AA** — Anti-androgen. A medication that blocks or reduces testosterone.

**AFAB** — Assigned Female At Birth.

**AMAB** — Assigned Male At Birth.

**Androgen** — A type of hormone that promotes masculine characteristics (e.g., testosterone, DHT).

**Aromatase** — An enzyme that converts testosterone into estradiol.

**Aromatase inhibitor** — A medication (anastrozole, letrozole) that blocks aromatase.

## B

**Bica / Bicalutamide** — A non-steroidal androgen receptor blocker.

**Bottom growth** — Clitoral enlargement from testosterone therapy.

**Buccal** — Absorbed through the gum/cheek.

## C

**CBC** — Complete blood count. Measures red/white blood cells and platelets.

**CPA / Cyproterone acetate** — A progestogenic anti-androgen.

**CMP** — Comprehensive metabolic panel. Tests liver/kidney function and electrolytes.

**Cypionate** — An ester (e.g., T cypionate, estradiol cypionate) that slows release of the hormone.

## D

**DHT** — Dihydrotestosterone. A potent androgen converted from T.

**DVT** — Deep vein thrombosis. A blood clot in a deep vein.

**DIY** — Do-It-Yourself. Obtaining HRT without a prescription.

## E

**E1** — Estrone. A weak estrogen produced by the liver.

**E2** — Estradiol. The primary active estrogen.

**EC** — Estradiol cypionate.

**EEn** — Estradiol enanthate.

**EV** — Estradiol valerate.

## F

**FSH** — Follicle-stimulating hormone. Part of the HPG axis.

**Free T** — Testosterone not bound to SHBG; the biologically active fraction.

## G

**GAHT** — Gender-Affirming Hormone Therapy.

**GnRH** — Gonadotropin-releasing hormone. Controls LH/FSH release.

**GnRH agonist** — A medication (leuprolide, goserelin) that shuts down LH/FSH production.

## H

**HCT / Hematocrit** — Percentage of red blood cells in blood. Monitored in T therapy.

**Homebrew** — Hormones made by community members rather than pharmaceutical companies.

**HPG axis** — Hypothalamus–Pituitary–Gonadal axis. The hormonal feedback loop that controls sex hormone production.

## I

**IM** — Intramuscular (injection into muscle).

**Informed consent** — A model of care where treatment begins after signing an acknowledgement of risks, without requiring a therapist's letter.

## L

**LH** — Luteinising hormone. Stimulates testosterone production in testes.

## M

**Meningioma** — A (usually benign) brain tumour associated with high-dose CPA.

**Metoidioplasty** — A surgical procedure that releases the clitoris to create a small phallus.

**Monotherapy** — Using estrogen alone (without AA) for T suppression.

## P

**PE** — Pulmonary embolism. A blood clot in the lungs.

**Pellets** — Subcutaneous hormone implants lasting several months.

**Polycythemia** — High red blood cell count; in the context of T therapy this specifically refers to **secondary erythrocytosis** (elevated haematocrit/haemoglobin from testosterone-stimulated erythropoiesis), distinct from polycythemia vera (a myeloproliferative disorder).

**Prolactin** — A hormone that can rise with CPA use.

## S

**SC / SubQ** — Subcutaneous (injection into fat tissue).

**SHBG** — Sex hormone-binding globulin. Binds to sex hormones, regulating free levels.

**Spiro / Spironolactone** — A potassium-sparing diuretic used as an anti-androgen.

## T

**T** — Testosterone.

**Tanner stage** — A scale of breast development stages.

**Trough** — The lowest point of hormone levels in a dosing cycle (best time to test blood).

## V

**VTE** — Venous thromboembolism. Blood clots in veins.

## W

**WPATH** — World Professional Association for Transgender Health. Publishes the Standards of Care (SOC).
