## Online Support

- **7 Cups** — Free emotional support chat
- **Transgender support groups** — Facebook, Discord, local meetups
- **BetterHelp / Talkspace** — Online therapy (some have LGBTQ+ specialists)

## General Tips

- HRT improves mental health for most trans people, but can temporarily worsen mood
- Monitor your mental health regularly (1–10 scale weekly)
- Build a support network before you need it
- If you have depression history, CPA may not be the best AA choice
- Exercise, sleep, and social connection are important
