# Harm Reduction Groups

## What Are Harm Reduction Groups?

Harm reduction groups provide education, resources, and support to reduce the risks associated with DIY HRT. They operate on the principle that people should have access to safety information regardless of their access to medical care.

## Resources

| Organisation | Focus | Services |
|-------------|-------|----------|
| **Trans Harm Reduction** | General | Education, sourcing guidance |
| **Regional needle exchanges** | Injection safety | Free needles, safe disposal |
| **LGBTQ+ health clinics** | Comprehensive care | Low-barrier HRT, blood tests |
| **Online communities** | Peer support | Advice, reviews, emotional support |

## How to Find Local Groups

- Search "LGBTQ+ health [your city]"
- Call local LGBTQ+ community centres
- Ask in trans Discord/Telegram groups
- Check with local universities (many have LGBTQ+ student groups)
