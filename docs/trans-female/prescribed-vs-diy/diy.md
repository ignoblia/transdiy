# DIY HRT (Trans Female)

## What Is DIY HRT?

DIY (Do-It-Yourself) HRT means obtaining and administering feminising hormones without a prescription or direct medical supervision. This typically involves:

- Ordering medications from online pharmacies or homebrew suppliers
- Self-determining dosage based on community knowledge and published guidelines
- Self-monitoring via private blood tests
- Managing side effects and dose adjustments independently

## Why People Choose DIY HRT

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| **Waiting lists** | Prescribed care can take years to access in many countries |
| **Gatekeeping** | Some providers impose unnecessary barriers or have outdated views |
| **Cost** | Private care can be prohibitively expensive; DIY is often cheaper |
| **Control** | Full control over your regimen, dosing, and choice of medications |
| **Stealth** | Avoids having a formal diagnosis on medical records |
| **Access** | Injectable estradiol or specific anti-androgens may not be available locally |

## Common DIY Medications

| Medication | Typical Dose | Notes |
|------------|-------------|-------|
| Estradiol valerate (injectable) | 2–8 mg every 5 days | Most common DIY injectable |
| Estradiol enanthate (injectable) | 3–8 mg every 7 days | Longer half-life than EV |
| Estradiol cypionate (injectable) | 1–5 mg every 7 days | Longer half-life than EV; lower dosing needed |
| Estradiol hemihydrate (pills) | 2–8 mg/day (sublingual preferred) | Bioidentical estradiol |
| Estradiol gel | 1–4 mg/day (2–8 pumps) | Transdermal, steady levels |
| Bicalutamide | 25–50 mg/day | Androgen receptor blocker |
| Cyproterone acetate | 10–12.5 mg/day | Testosterone production suppressor |
| Progesterone (micronised) | 100–200 mg/day (oral or rectal) | Optional, for breast maturation |

## Risks of DIY HRT

| Risk | Details | Mitigation |
|------|---------|------------|
| **No medical oversight** | No professional to catch issues early | Regular blood tests (every 3 months min) |
| **Variable product quality** | Risk of under/over-dosed or counterfeit products | Use trusted, verified sources |
| **Incorrect dosing** | Too high = increased risk; too low = no results | Follow established guidelines; test levels |
| **Blood clot risk (VTE)** | Particularly with oral estrogen at high doses | Use transdermal/injectable; avoid smoking |
| **Legal consequences** | Importing prescription meds may be illegal | Know your country's laws |
| **No fertility preservation** | Many don't consider this before starting | Bank sperm before starting if desired |
| **Contamination (homebrew)** | Homebrew injectables carry infection risk | Use sterile technique; buy from reputable brewers |

## Requirements for Safe DIY HRT

::: danger Minimum requirements for DIY HRT
1. **Blood tests every 3 months** — at minimum E2, T, SHBG, CBC, metabolic panel
2. **Validated sources** — from the community-vetted vendor list (see Sources section)
3. **Dose education** — understand starting doses, adjustment principles, and target ranges
4. **Emergency awareness** — know the signs of DVT, pulmonary embolism, stroke, and meningioma
5. **Backup plan** — have a plan for if you need to stop or switch to prescribed care
:::

::: tip Recommended but not strictly required
- Baseline blood test **before** starting HRT (to establish your natural levels)
- Blood pressure monitor at home
- Knowledge of your family medical history (clotting disorders, breast cancer, etc.)
- A supportive GP or clinic you can access in an emergency
:::

| Item | DIY Cost Range | Prescribed Cost Range |
