# Legal Risks of DIY HRT by Country

## General Principles

The legal status of DIY HRT varies enormously by country. In most places, the laws governing hormones were not written with trans people in mind — they are general laws about prescription medications, controlled substances, and unlicensed medical practice.

::: warning I am not a lawyer
This page is a practical overview based on community knowledge and publicly available legal information. It does **not** constitute legal advice. Consult a lawyer in your jurisdiction for definitive guidance.
:::

### Key Legal Questions

1. **Is estrogen a controlled substance?** — In almost all countries, bioidentical estradiol (estradiol valerate, hemihydrate, etc.) is **not** a controlled substance. It is a prescription medicine.
2. **Is testosterone a controlled substance?** — **Yes**, in most countries. Testosterone is classified as a controlled substance (anabolic steroid) in the US, UK, EU, Australia, Canada, and many other jurisdictions.
3. **Is importing prescription medication without a prescription illegal?** — Often, yes. However, enforcement varies widely.
4. **Is personal possession without a prescription illegal?** — This varies. Some countries allow personal use of non-controlled prescription medications.

## Country-by-Country Overview

### United States

| Aspect | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Estradiol | Prescription medication; **not** a controlled substance |
| Spironolactone | Prescription medication; not controlled |
| Bicalutamide | Prescription medication; not controlled |
| Cyproterone acetate | Not FDA-approved; importation is technically illegal but rarely enforced |
| Importation for personal use | FDA generally tolerates up to a 90-day supply for personal use with a valid prescription |
| Without prescription | Technically illegal, but enforcement against individuals buying from overseas pharmacies is extremely rare |
| Testosterone (for trans men) | **Schedule III controlled substance** — importation carries real legal risk |

**Key resources:**
- FDA guidance on personal importation
- Planned Parenthood for low-barrier prescribed HRT

### United Kingdom

| Aspect | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Estradiol | Prescription-only medicine (POM); **not** a controlled substance |
| Cyproterone acetate | Prescription-only; not controlled |
| Importation | Personal importation of non-controlled prescription meds for personal use is a grey area — technically a criminal offence under the Human Medicines Regulations 2012, but prosecutions for personal use are rare |
| Practical risk | **Low** for estradiol-only; customs may seize packages but rarely pursue charges |
| **Important** | The UK has extremely long NHS waiting lists (5–7+ years), making DIY a very common choice |

### European Union

| Aspect | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Estradiol | Prescription medication; not controlled |
| Intra-EU shipping | Medications purchased from another EU country for personal use are generally permitted |
| Outside EU | Importation rules vary by member state |
| Czech Republic, Poland | Some medications available OTC or with very low prescription barriers |
| Germany, France | Strictly prescription-only; importation carries some risk |
| Netherlands | CPA requires a prescription; estradiol is prescription-only |

### Canada

| Aspect | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Estradiol | Prescription medication; not controlled |
| Importation | Health Canada allows personal importation of up to a 90-day supply of non-controlled prescription drugs **if** you have a prescription |
| Without prescription | Technically illegal, but enforcement against individuals for non-controlled substances is rare |
| Practical risk | **Low** — many Canadians DIY while on waitlists |

### Australia

| Aspect | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Estradiol | Prescription medication (Schedule 4); not controlled |
| Importation | Personal importation of up to 3 months supply is allowed under certain conditions (without a prescription for Schedule 4 drugs, it's technically illegal) |
| Practical risk | **Low to moderate** — TGA may seize shipments but rarely prosecutes individuals |
| Testosterone | **Schedule 4** (prescription-only) but also an **anabolic steroid** — importing carries higher risk |

### New Zealand

| Aspect | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Estradiol | Prescription medication; not controlled |
| Importation | Medsafe generally tolerates personal import for non-controlled meds |
| Practical risk | **Low** |

### Japan

| Aspect | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Estradiol | Prescription medication |
| Importation | Importing without a prescription is illegal and can result in seizure; risk is higher than Western countries |
| Practical risk | **Moderate** — customs is strict; consider using domestic grey-market sources |

### Brazil

| Aspect | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Estradiol | Some forms available over the counter (Oestrogel, certain pills) |
| Practical risk | **Low** — easy access to estradiol without prescription |

### Russia

| Aspect | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Estradiol | Prescription required in theory; many pharmacies sell without prescription in practice |
| Practical risk | **Low to moderate** — enforcement is inconsistent; anti-trans laws are a growing concern |

### India

| Aspect | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Estradiol | Widely available OTC at pharmacies |
| Practical risk | **Low** — many generic manufacturers based in India |

### China

| Aspect | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Estradiol | Prescription required; online purchasing without prescription is common |
| Practical risk | **Low to moderate** — inconsistent enforcement |

## General Risk Reduction Strategies

| Strategy | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Order small quantities** | Smaller packages attract less customs attention |
| **Use domestic sources** | Avoids customs entirely |
| **Discreet packaging** | Many vendors offer plain packaging |
| **Know your country's limits** | Some allow 30/90-day personal importation |
| **Have a backup story** | If questioned, some say it's "vitamins" or "research chemicals" (but lying to customs carries its own risks) |
| **Use crypto payments** | Avoids bank records of HRT purchases |
| **Keep packages small** | Multiple small shipments are less suspicious than one large one |

## What to Do If a Package Is Seized

1. **Do not claim the package** — if customs sends a letter, ignoring it is often safer than responding
2. **Do not sign for anything** — refuse delivery if asked to sign
3. **Do not volunteer information** — if questioned, say nothing and request a lawyer
4. **Destroy evidence** — delete purchase records, wipe browser history
5. **Have a backup supply** — always keep a buffer stock in case of shipping delays or seizures
