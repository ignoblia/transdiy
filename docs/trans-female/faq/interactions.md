# Interactions with Other Medications

## Overview

HRT medications can interact with other drugs. Some interactions are minor, while others can be dangerous. Always check for interactions when starting a new medication.

## Estrogen Interactions

| Medication | Interaction | Severity | Action |
|------------|-------------|----------|--------|
| **Anticonvulsants** (phenytoin, carbamazepine) | Reduce estrogen effectiveness | ⚠️ Moderate | May need higher E2 dose |
| **Rifampin** (antibiotic) | Reduces estrogen levels | ⚠️ Moderate | Monitor E2 levels |
| **St. John's Wort** | Reduces estrogen effectiveness | ⚠️ Moderate | Avoid if possible |
| **Corticosteroids** | Estrogen increases corticosteroid levels | ⚠️ Moderate | Monitor for Cushing's symptoms |
| **Thyroid medication** | Estrogen increases TBG; may need higher thyroid dose | ⚠️ Moderate | Monitor TSH |
| **HIV protease inhibitors** | Can alter E2 levels | ⚠️ Moderate | Monitor E2 |

## Spironolactone Interactions

| Medication | Interaction | Severity | Action |
|------------|-------------|----------|--------|
| **ACE inhibitors** (lisinopril, ramipril) | Risk of hyperkalemia (high potassium) | 🔴 Serious | Monitor potassium closely |
| **ARBs** (losartan, valsartan) | Risk of hyperkalemia | 🔴 Serious | Monitor potassium closely |
| **NSAIDs** (ibuprofen, naproxen, diclofenac) | Reduced kidney function; hyperkalemia risk | ⚠️ Moderate | Avoid chronic use; monitor potassium |
| **Potassium supplements** | Hyperkalemia risk | 🔴 Serious | Do NOT take potassium supplements |
| **Lithium** | Spiro increases lithium levels | ⚠️ Moderate | Monitor lithium levels |
| **Digoxin** | Spiro increases digoxin levels | ⚠️ Moderate | Monitor digoxin levels |
| **Other diuretics** | Excessive electrolyte loss | ⚠️ Moderate | Avoid combination |

## CPA (Cyproterone Acetate) Interactions

| Medication | Interaction | Severity | Action |
|------------|-------------|----------|--------|
| **Alcohol** | CPA increases alcohol sensitivity | ⚠️ Moderate | Limit alcohol intake |
| **Antidepressants** (SSRIs) | Potential additive effect on depression | ⚠️ Moderate | Monitor mood closely |
| **Statins** | CPA may increase statin levels | ⚠️ Mild | Monitor if concerned |
| **Insulin / diabetes meds** | CPA can affect blood sugar | ⚠️ Moderate | Monitor blood glucose |

## Bicalutamide Interactions

| Medication | Interaction | Severity |
|------------|-------------|----------|
| **Warfarin** | Bicalutamide may increase warfarin effect | ⚠️ Moderate |
| **Statins** | Possible increased liver enzyme elevation | ⚠️ Mild |
| **Hepatotoxic drugs** | Additive liver stress | ⚠️ Moderate |

## Progesterone Interactions

| Medication | Interaction | Severity |
|------------|-------------|----------|
| **CYP3A4 inducers** (rifampin, some anticonvulsants) | Reduce progesterone effectiveness | ⚠️ Moderate |
| **Benzodiazepines** | Additive sedation (especially oral progesterone) | ⚠️ Moderate |

## General Precautions

- **Always inform your doctor** (and pharmacist) that you are on HRT
- **Check interactions** using a reliable tool (Drugs.com interaction checker)
- **Start new medications one at a time** so you can identify problems
- **Monitor blood work** more frequently when adding interacting meds
- **Be cautious with alcohol** — HRT can change your tolerance
