# Traveling with HRT

## General Principles

| Principle | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| **Carry medications in hand luggage** | Checked luggage can be lost or delayed |
| **Keep in original packaging** | Makes it easier to identify the medication |
| **Bring a copy of your prescription** | Even if it's DIY — a prescription from your doctor helps |
| **Know the laws of your destination** | HRT legality varies widely |
| **Don't carry more than you need** | 90-day supply or less is less likely to raise questions |

## Airport Security

### Domestic Travel

- Estradiol is **not a controlled substance** in most countries — no special restrictions
- Keep medications in original labelled packaging
- Gel, injectables, and needles may get additional screening
- If asked, say "it's prescription hormone therapy"

### International Travel

| Situation | Likely Outcome |
|-----------|---------------|
| Estradiol pills (carrying personal supply) | Very unlikely to be questioned |
| Estradiol gel (larger bottles) | May be screened; have documentation |
| Injectables (vials + needles) | Likely to be inspected; bring prescription |
| Anti-androgens (pills) | Unlikely to be questioned |

### Security Screening

- **Liquids/gels:** Estradiol gel may count toward the 100 mL liquids limit
- **Needles:** If carrying injectable supplies, declare them at security
- **Pat-downs:** You may be patted down; state your gender marker if relevant
- **Screening machines:** Body scanners may flag chest areas for trans women — you can request a pat-down instead

## Traveling with Injectables

| Item | TSA/UK/EU Rules |
|------|-----------------|
| Vials | No restriction on quantity for personal medical use |
| Syringes/needles | Allowed with medication; declare at security |
| Sharps container | Recommended for used needles |
| Ice pack | If medication needs refrigeration |

## Crossing Borders with DIY HRT

::: warning Know the laws of your destination
Some countries have strict medication importation laws. Research before you travel. For most destinations, a personal supply (30–90 days) of non-controlled medications is fine, but rules vary.
:::

### Countries with Higher Risk

- **Japan** — strict medication importation laws
- **United Arab Emirates** — strict medication controls; estradiol may be restricted
- **Russia** — anti-trans laws are a concern
- **Saudi Arabia** — strict medication importation

### Low-Risk Countries

- **EU countries** — generally fine with personal supply
- **US, Canada, Australia, NZ** — fine for non-controlled substances
- **UK** — fine for personal supply

## HRT While Traveling Long-Term

If you'll be away for more than 90 days:

- Have enough medication for the trip (or a way to receive shipments)
- Consider using a longer-acting formulation (EEn injections, implants)
- Know where to find a pharmacy in your destination
- Have a backup plan if your medication is lost or seized

## Pro Tips

- **Carry a doctor's note** on letterhead stating your medication is for personal use
- **Take photos** of your prescription and medications before you go
- **Split your supply** between hand luggage and checked luggage (as backup)
- **Check the International Narcotics Control Board** website for destination-specific rules (for controlled substances)
- **Join a local trans community group** at your destination — they'll know the local laws
