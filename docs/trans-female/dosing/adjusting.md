# Adjusting Doses

## Principles of Dose Adjustment

1. **Change one thing at a time** — never adjust estrogen and anti-androgen simultaneously
2. **Wait 4–8 weeks between changes** — it takes time for levels to stabilise
3. **Always test before adjusting** — base decisions on blood tests, not guesses
4. **Change by small increments** — 1 mg of E2 or 12.5–25 mg of spiro at a time
5. **Track symptoms** — levels aren't the only thing that matters; how you feel matters too

## When to Adjust Estrogen

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| E2 < 100 pg/mL (trough) and T > 50 ng/dL | **Increase E2 dose** |
| E2 100–200 pg/mL, T < 50 ng/dL, no issues | **Maintain current dose** |
| E2 > 300 pg/mL (trough) | **Consider reducing dose** — no added benefit, may increase risks |
| E2 < 100 pg/mL but T < 50 ng/dL | **Potentially increase E2** for feminisation; T is already suppressed |
| E2 > 200 pg/mL but T > 50 ng/dL | **Increase AA or switch AA** before increasing E2 further |
| SHBG > 150 nmol/L | E2 dose may be too high; reducing E2 may improve free T suppression |

## When to Adjust Anti-Androgen

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| T > 50 ng/dL (on current AA) | Increase AA dose (if below max) or switch AA |
| T < 50 ng/dL with side effects | Consider reducing AA or switching to lower-side-effect AA |
| T < 30 ng/dL (very suppressed) | Can try reducing AA dose or transitioning to monotherapy |
| Side effects intolerable | Switch to a different AA class |

## Sample Adjustment Scenarios

### Scenario 1: E2 Too Low, T Not Suppressed

**Current:** Estradiol 2 mg oral/day, Spiro 50 mg/day
**Levels:** E2 = 45 pg/mL, T = 150 ng/dL

**Plan:**
1. Increase estradiol to 4 mg/day (2 mg twice daily) or switch to sublingual
2. Re-test in 6–8 weeks
3. If still insufficient, increase spiro to 100 mg/day

### Scenario 2: E2 OK, T Still High

**Current:** Estradiol 4 mg/day, Spiro 100 mg/day
**Levels:** E2 = 120 pg/mL, T = 80 ng/dL

**Plan:**
1. Increase spiro to 150 mg/day, or switch to CPA 12.5 mg/day or bicalutamide 50 mg/day
2. Re-test in 6–8 weeks

### Scenario 3: E2 High, T Suppressed on Low AA

**Current:** EV 6 mg every 5 days
**Levels:** E2 = 350 pg/mL (trough), T = 15 ng/dL

**Plan:**
1. Consider reducing EV to 4 mg every 5 days
2. T is already well-suppressed — no AA needed if this is monotherapy
3. Monitor SHBG — if >150 nmol/L, definitely reduce E2 dose
4. Recent evidence shows doses above 4-5 mg weekly often produce supraphysiologic levels without added T suppression benefit

### Scenario 4: Everything in Range, But Poor Results

**Current:** E2 = 150 pg/mL, T = 20 ng/dL, but minimal feminisation after 12+ months

**Plan:**
1. Check SHBG — if very high, E2 may need to be lowered
2. Check DHT (dihydrotestosterone) — some people convert T to DHT even with low T
3. Consider adding progesterone (if not already)
4. Consider switching E2 route (e.g., oral to injectable)
5. Consider that genetics and time play a bigger role than levels

## Common Pitfalls

| Mistake | Why It's Wrong |
|---------|----------------|
| Adjusting every 2 weeks | Levels need 4–8 weeks to stabilise |
| Increasing E2 when T is the problem | T suppression needs AA adjustment, not more E2 (usually) |
| Increasing both E2 and AA at once | You won't know which change caused the result |
| Chasing "optimal" levels obsessively | Being in range is good enough; more is not better |
| Not testing at trough | Results are meaningless without consistent timing |

## Advanced: The SHBG Problem

SHBG (sex hormone-binding globulin) binds to free sex hormones, making them inactive. High SHBG can reduce free estradiol and free testosterone even when total levels look good.

| SHBG Level | Interpretation |
|------------|---------------|
| 40–120 nmol/L | Normal range for trans women on HRT |
| 120–150 nmol/L | Moderate elevation — monitor |
| > 150 nmol/L | Significantly elevated — may reduce free hormone activity |

High SHBG is often caused by **high oral estrogen doses**. Switching to transdermal or injectable E2 can lower SHBG and free up more active hormone.
