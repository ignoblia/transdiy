# Interpreting Your Results

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Check Your E2 Level

| Result | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| < 50 pg/mL | Too low — increase E2 dose |
| 50–100 pg/mL | Low — consider increasing |
| **100–200 pg/mL** | ✅ **Target range** |
| 200–300 pg/mL | Acceptable (especially for monotherapy) |
| > 300 pg/mL (trough) | High — consider reducing if SHBG is elevated |

### Step 2: Check Your T Level

| Result | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| > 200 ng/dL | Not suppressed — increase AA or E2 |
| 100–200 ng/dL | Partially suppressed — increase AA |
| 50–100 ng/dL | Borderline — consider increase |
| **< 50 ng/dL** | ✅ **Target range** |
| < 20 ng/dL | Fully suppressed — consider reducing AA if side effects |

### Step 3: Check SHBG

| Result | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 40–120 nmol/L | Normal range for trans women on HRT |
| 120–150 nmol/L | Moderate elevation — monitor |
| > 150 nmol/L | High — consider switching from oral to transdermal/injectable E2 |

## Case Examples

### Case 1: New Starter, Good Results

```
E2: 120 pg/mL    ✅
T:  40 ng/dL     ✅
SHBG: 55 nmol/L  ✅
```

**Assessment:** Great levels! Continue current regimen. Re-test in 3 months.

### Case 2: E2 Too Low, T Not Suppressed

```
E2: 45 pg/mL     ❌ Low
T:  180 ng/dL    ❌ Not suppressed
SHBG: 30 nmol/L  ✅
```

**Assessment:** Estrogen dose is too low. Increase E2 first (e.g., from 2 mg to 4 mg oral). Re-test in 6–8 weeks.

### Case 3: Good E2, T Not Suppressed

```
E2: 150 pg/mL    ✅
T:  85 ng/dL     ❌ Borderline
SHBG: 60 nmol/L  ✅
```

**Assessment:** E2 is fine but T needs more suppression. Increase anti-androgen (e.g., spiro 100 → 150 mg/day, or switch to CPA/bica).

### Case 4: High SHBG Problem

```
E2: 200 pg/mL    ✅
T:  30 ng/dL     ✅
SHBG: 180 nmol/L ❌ High
```

**Assessment:** E2 and T look good, but SHBG is high — likely from oral estrogen. Consider switching to transdermal or injectable E2 to lower SHBG and free up more active hormones.

### Case 5: Monotherapy Working

```
E2: 250 pg/mL    ✅ (acceptable for monotherapy)
T:  25 ng/dL     ✅
SHBG: 80 nmol/L  ✅
Regimen: EV 6 mg every 5 days, no AA
```

**Assessment:** Monotherapy is working well. Maintain current dose. No anti-androgen needed.

### Case 6: High E2 but T Not Suppressed

```
E2: 320 pg/mL    ❌ High trough
T:  70 ng/dL     ❌ Not suppressed
SHBG: 140 nmol/L ⚠️ Elevated
Regimen: EV 10 mg every 5 days, no AA
```

**Assessment:** Very high E2 dose with inadequate T suppression. Adding an AA (e.g., bicalutamide 25–50 mg) would be better than increasing E2 further. Also consider reducing E2 slightly.

## Troubleshooting Guide

### "My E2 is in range but I see no changes"

| Possible Cause | Next Step |
|----------------|-----------|
| High SHBG | Check SHBG; switch from oral to transdermal/injectable |
| Too early | HRT takes years — are you < 6 months in? |
| Genetics | Some people are slow responders |
| Low free E2 | Check free E2 (requires special test) |
| DHT still high | Add DHT test; consider finasteride if elevated |

### "My T is not going down"

| Possible Cause | Next Step |
|----------------|-----------|
| AA dose too low | Increase spiro to 200 mg or switch to CPA/bica |
| E2 dose too low (for monotherapy) | Increase injectable E2 |
| Non-adherence | Are you taking meds consistently? |
| Testosterone production issue | Check LH; if suppressed, consider DHT or adrenal source |

### "My prolactin is elevated"

| Level | Action |
|-------|--------|
| < 25 ng/mL | Normal — no action needed |
| 25–50 ng/mL | Mild elevation — monitor; recheck in 3 months |
| 50–100 ng/mL | Moderate elevation — consider switching off CPA if on it; rule out other causes |
| > 100 ng/mL | Significant elevation — stop CPA; consider pituitary MRI |
| > 200 ng/mL | High concern — likely prolactinoma; seek endocrinology consult |

## Common Patterns and Solutions

| Pattern | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| High E2, high SHBG, normal T | Oral estrogen | Switch to transdermal/injectable |
| Normal E2, high T | AA insufficient | Increase or switch AA |
| Normal E2, normal T, no changes | SHBG high or genetics | Check SHBG; be patient |
| High E2, normal T, no AA | Monotherapy working | Nothing — this is the goal! |
