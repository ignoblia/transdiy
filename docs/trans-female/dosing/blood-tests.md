# Blood Tests — What & When

## Why Blood Tests Are Essential

Blood tests are the **only reliable way** to know if your HRT is working correctly. Without them, you are guessing. Regular testing allows you to:

- Confirm your E2 is in feminising range (100–200 pg/mL)
- Confirm your T is suppressed (< 50 ng/dL)
- Monitor for side effects (liver, kidney, prolactin, lipids)
- Make informed dose adjustments

## Before Starting HRT (Baseline)

::: danger Get a baseline blood test before taking any hormones
A baseline establishes your natural hormone levels and health markers. Without it, you can't measure how much HRT is changing things.
:::

| Test | Why It Matters |
|------|----------------|
| **E2 (estradiol)** | Natural baseline level |
| **Total T (testosterone)** | Natural baseline level |
| **LH, FSH** | HPG axis function |
| **SHBG** | Hormone-binding protein baseline |
| **CBC (complete blood count)** | Red/white blood cells, platelets |
| **Comprehensive metabolic panel** | Liver (ALT, AST), kidney (creatinine), electrolytes |
| **Lipid panel** | Cholesterol, triglycerides |
| **Prolactin** | Baseline before CPA |
| **Blood pressure** | Separate from blood test but equally important |

## First Year of HRT (Every 3 Months)

| Test | Target | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| **E2** | 100–200 pg/mL **(367–734 pmol/L)** (trough) | Draw blood just before next dose |
| **Total T** | < 50 ng/dL **(< 1.73 nmol/L)** | |
| **SHBG** | < 150 nmol/L | If elevated, consider route change |
| **CBC** | Normal range | Anaemia monitoring |
| **CMP** | Normal range | Liver/kidney function |
| **Potassium** (if on spiro) | Normal range | Check every 3 months in year 1 |

## After First Year (Every 6–12 Months)

| Test | Frequency |
|------|-----------|
| E2, T, SHBG | Every 6 months |
| CBC, CMP | Annually |
| Lipid panel | Annually |
| Prolactin (if on CPA) | Annually |
| Potassium (if on spiro) | Annually (if stable) |

## How to Get Blood Tests

### With a Prescribing Provider

Your provider will order labs as part of routine care. This is the best option — tests are covered by insurance/public healthcare.

### Without a Provider (DIY)

| Method | How It Works | Cost |
|--------|-------------|------|
| **Private lab (US)** | Order online from LabCorp/Quest via sites like PrivateMDLabs or Jason Health | $50–150 |
| **GP request (UK)** | Some GPs will order blood tests under a harm reduction agreement even if they won't prescribe | Free (NHS) |
| **Walk-in clinic** | Some clinics will order tests if you explain you're self-managing HRT | Varies |
| **Finger-prick tests** | Home test kits for E2, T (less accurate than venous draw) | $30–80 |

## Tips for Accurate Results

::: danger TEST AT TROUGH — NOT PEAK
For injectable hormones, blood must be drawn **immediately before your next dose** (trough).
- **Estradiol injections:** trough = just before next injection (e.g., day 5 or 7)
- **Testosterone injections:** trough = just before next injection (typically day 7)

**Do not test within 48 hours of an injection.** Testing at peak gives misleadingly high results and can cause you to lower your dose incorrectly.
:::

| Tip | Why |
|-----|-----|
| **Test at trough** | Just before your next dose/application — shows minimum levels |
| **Consistent timing** | Always test at the same point in your cycle |
| **Same lab** | Different labs use different assays; consistency matters |
| **Morning draw** | T levels are highest in the morning; test then for consistency |
| **Fast (optional)** | Fasting is recommended for lipid panels |
| **No biotin** | Biotin supplements can falsely alter hormone test results; stop 3–5 days before |

## Understanding Your Lab Results

### Units Conversion

| Hormone | Common Units | Conversion |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| Estradiol (E2) | pg/mL | 1 pg/mL = 3.67 pmol/L |
| Testosterone (T) | ng/dL | 1 ng/dL = 0.0347 nmol/L |
| SHBG | nmol/L | No conversion needed |

### What the Numbers Mean

### Estradiol (E2)

| Level | Interpretation |
|-------|---------------|
| < 50 pg/mL | Too low; no feminisation |
| 50–100 pg/mL | Low; some effect but likely insufficient |
| **100–200 pg/mL** | **Target range** |
| 200–300 pg/mL | High but acceptable (common with monotherapy) |
| > 300 pg/mL (trough) | Very high; consider reducing dose |

### Total Testosterone (T)

| Level | Interpretation |
|-------|---------------|
| > 200 ng/dL | Male range; HRT not working |
| 100–200 ng/dL | Partially suppressed; not enough |
| 50–100 ng/dL | Borderline; some people do well here |
| **< 50 ng/dL** | **Target range** |
| < 20 ng/dL | Fully suppressed; acceptable |
