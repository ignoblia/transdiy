# Dosage Calculators

## Overview

There is no perfect "calculator" for HRT dosing — individual responses vary too much. However, the following tables provide starting point equivalencies and dose adjustment guidelines.

## Estrogen Dose Equivalencies

> **Note:** These are population averages. Many people achieve target levels with lower doses (e.g., 3–4 mg/week of EV). Always start low and adjust based on blood tests.

Approximate equivalent doses for achieving similar E2 levels:

| Oral Estradiol | Sublingual | Transdermal Patch | Gel | Injectable EV | Injectable EC | Injectable EEn |
|---------------|------------|-------------------|-----|---------------|---------------|----------------|
| 2 mg/day | 1 mg/day | 50 mcg/24h | 2 pumps (~1.5 mg) | 2 mg/5 days | 2 mg/7 days | 4 mg/7 days |
| 4 mg/day | 2 mg/day | 100 mcg/24h | 4 pumps (~3 mg) | 4 mg/5 days | 3 mg/7 days | 5 mg/7 days |
| 6 mg/day | 3 mg/day | 150 mcg/24h | 6 pumps (~4.5 mg) | 5 mg/5 days | 4 mg/7 days | 6 mg/7 days |
| 8 mg/day | 4 mg/day | 200 mcg/24h | 8 pumps (~6 mg) | 6 mg/5 days | 5 mg/7 days | 8 mg/7 days |

## Injection Dose Calculator

### Estradiol Valerate (EV)

| Desired Trough E2 | Dose (every 5 days) | Notes |
|-------------------|---------------------|-------|
| 100 pg/mL (367 pmol/L) | 2–3 mg | Below typical monotherapy threshold |
| 150 pg/mL (551 pmol/L) | 3–4 mg | May still need AA for T suppression |
| 200 pg/mL (734 pmol/L) | 4–6 mg | Monotherapy target for most people |
| 250–300 pg/mL (918–1,101 pmol/L) | 5–8 mg | Higher end rarely needed |

### Estradiol Cypionate (EC)

| Desired Trough E2 | Dose (every 7 days) | Notes |
|-------------------|---------------------|-------|
| 100 pg/mL (367 pmol/L) | 2–3 mg | Below typical monotherapy threshold |
| 150 pg/mL (551 pmol/L) | 2–4 mg | May still need AA for T suppression |
| 200 pg/mL (734 pmol/L) | 3–5 mg | Monotherapy target for most people |
| 250–300 pg/mL (918–1,101 pmol/L) | 4–6 mg | Higher end rarely needed |

### Estradiol Enanthate (EEn)

| Desired Trough E2 | Dose (every 7 days) | Notes |
|-------------------|---------------------|-------|
| 100 pg/mL (367 pmol/L) | 3–4 mg | Below typical monotherapy threshold |
| 150 pg/mL (551 pmol/L) | 4–5 mg | May still need AA for T suppression |
| 200 pg/mL (734 pmol/L) | 4–6 mg | Monotherapy target for most people |
| 250–300 pg/mL (918–1,101 pmol/L) | 6–8 mg | Higher end rarely needed |

> Recent evidence: Multisite studies (2024-2025) found the median effective weekly dose for achieving both target E2 and T suppression is around 4 mg (IQR 3-6 mg). Doses above 6 mg/week consistently produce supraphysiologic E2 levels in most people. Start low and titrate based on blood tests.
> 
> Note on EC: Estradiol cypionate has a longer hydrocarbon tail than EV, making it more lipophilic with a longer duration and smaller peaks. Doses ≤5 mg weekly are typically sufficient. UCSF guidelines recommend a maximum of 5 mg/week for EC, compared to 20 mg/week for EV.

## Concentration Calculations

Injectable estradiol comes in different concentrations. Always check your vial concentration.

| Concentration | 1 mg = | 5 mg = | 10 mg = |
|---------------|--------|--------|---------|
| 10 mg/mL | 0.1 mL | 0.5 mL | 1.0 mL |
| 20 mg/mL | 0.05 mL | 0.25 mL | 0.5 mL |
| 40 mg/mL | 0.025 mL | 0.125 mL | 0.25 mL |

### Formula

```
Volume to inject (mL) = Desired dose (mg) ÷ Vial concentration (mg/mL)

Example: You want 5 mg of EV from a 20 mg/mL vial
5 mg ÷ 20 mg/mL = 0.25 mL
```

## Estimating Whether You Need an AA

A rough guide: if your E2 trough is:

| E2 Trough | Likely T Suppression Without AA |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| < 100 pg/mL | Possible (~43% achieve T < 50 ng/dL per Mount Sinai study) |
| 100–200 pg/mL | Likely (~75% achieve T < 50 ng/dL) |
| 200–300 pg/mL | Very likely (~89% achieve T < 50 ng/dL) |
| > 300 pg/mL | Very likely but supraphysiologic; rarely needed |

## Remember: These Are Starting Points

- **Individual response varies widely** — one person's 4 mg EV may be another person's 8 mg
- **Always test blood levels** 4–8 weeks after any dose change
- **Adjust based on levels, not calculations**
- **More is not always better** — recent evidence shows doses above 6 mg weekly consistently produce supraphysiologic E2 levels without additional T suppression benefit
- **Start low and go slow** — 3-5 mg weekly is a reasonable starting range for injectable E2
