# Homebrew Suppliers

## What Is Homebrew?

Homebrew refers to estradiol injectable solutions made by community members (not pharmaceutical companies). These are typically:

- **Estradiol esters** (EV, EEn, EC) dissolved in **carrier oil** (MCT, grapeseed, sesame, etc.)
- **Vial concentrations** ranging from 10–50 mg/mL
- Sold through community channels (Reddit, Discord, Telegram)

## Why People Use Homebrew

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| **Cost** | Homebrew is significantly cheaper than pharmaceutical injectables |
| **Access** | Injectable estradiol is hard to get a prescription for in many countries |
| **Custom concentrations** | Can get higher concentrations (40 mg/mL) that reduce injection volume |
| **Ester options** | EEn and EC are easier to source via homebrew than pharma |

## How to Evaluate a Homebrew Supplier

| Factor | What to Look For |
|--------|------------------|
| **Community reputation** | Long-standing positive reviews on Reddit/Telegram |
| **Sterile practices** | Labelled with batch number, ingredients, concentration, date |
| **Transparency** | Willing to share brewing process and sources of raw materials |
| **Testing** | Some suppliers send samples for third-party HPLC testing |
| **Packaging** | Sealed vials, sterile crimp caps, proper labelling |

## Risks of Homebrew

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Contamination** (bacteria, endotoxins) | Choose suppliers with sterile protocols; inspect vials before use |
| **Inaccurate concentration** | Compare your blood test results to expected levels |
| **Allergic reaction** (to carrier oil) | Know what oil is used (MCT, grapeseed, sesame, etc.) |
| **No USP/PhEur standard** | Homebrew is not pharmaceutical grade |
| **Scams** (never ships, incorrect product) | Use trusted, community-verified suppliers |

## Sterile Technique When Using Homebrew

- Always wipe vial septum with 70% isopropyl alcohol before drawing
- Use a new needle and syringe for each injection
- Do not share vials with others
- Store at room temperature, away from light
- Discard if you see cloudiness, floating particles, or discolouration
