# Online Pharmacies

## Overview

Online pharmacies are a common source of HRT medications for trans women, especially those who cannot access prescribed care. This page covers how to use them safely.

## Types of Online Pharmacies

| Type | Description | Reliability |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Licensed pharmacies (require prescription)** | Legitimate pharmacies that ship internationally | ✅ High — but you need a prescription |
| **Online pharmacies (no prescription)** | Ship without requiring a prescription | ⚠️ Variable; depends on reputation |
| **Grey-market sellers** | Unofficial sellers of pharmaceutical-grade products | ⚠️ Use community-verified sources |
| **Marketplace listings** | eBay, Amazon, Alibaba sellers | ❌ High risk of counterfeits |

## How to Verify a Pharmacy

| Check | What to Look For |
|-------|------------------|
| **Community reputation** | Search for reviews on Reddit (r/TransDIY, r/AskMtFHRT), Discord |
| **Years in operation** | Established vendors are generally safer |
| **Payment methods accepted** | Legitimate vendors offer crypto, WISE, or PayPal |
| **Product packaging** | Photos of actual products with batch numbers |
| **Contact/communication** | Responsive customer service |
| **Transparency about manufacturing** | Willing to share source of products |

::: warning Red Flags
- Prices that seem too good to be true (they usually are)
- Requiring payment via untraceable methods only (Western Union, MoneyGram)
- Only accepting payment in risky cryptocurrencies (no buyer protection)
- No contact information or unresponsive customer service
- Many negative reviews or reports of under-dosed products
- Claiming to sell "pharmaceutical grade" but refusing to share testing or sourcing info
:::

## Popular Online Pharmacy Categories

### Estradiol (Pills, Patches, Gel)

- **Pharmaceutical-grade** from Indian or Turkish manufacturers is common
- Generic estradiol hemihydrate and estradiol valerate are widely available
- Patches and gel are more expensive than pills

### Injectable Estradiol

- **DIY/homebrew** — made by community members (most common for injectables)
- **Pharmaceutical** compounding pharmacies — require prescription, rarely ship internationally
- **Veterinary** — estradiol for animals (do NOT use — not sterile or accurately dosed)

### Anti-Androgens

| Medication | Online Availability | Notes |
|------------|-------------------|-------|
| Spironolactone | Widely available | Many generic manufacturers |
| CPA | Widely available (non-US) | Common in EU/Asian pharmacies |
| Bicalutamide | Available | More expensive than spiro/CPA |
| Finasteride | Widely available | Also sold for hair loss |

## Safety Tips

| Tip | Why |
|-----|-----|
| Start with a small test order | Verify quality before committing to large order |
| Check packaging and batch numbers | Legitimate products have clear labelling |
| Compare to pharmaceutical reference | Pills should look identical to known legitimate samples |
| Store properly | Heat/moisture degrade medications |
| Track your levels | Blood tests reveal under/over-dosed products |
| Keep backup supply | Shipping delays happen |

## What to Do If You Receive Suspect Medication

- **Do not use it**
- Post photos to community forums for verification
- Contact the seller (some will replace or refund)
- Report to the community to warn others

In some countries, HRT is available without a prescription, though suitable formulations for trans people can be hard to find, with some containing high-dose cyproterone acetate or non-bioidentical estrogens like ethinylestradiol.
