# Payment & Shipping Safely

## Payment Methods

| Method | Privacy | Buyer Protection | Notes |
|--------|---------|-----------------|-------|
| **Cryptocurrency** | ✅ High | ❌ None | Most common for DIY; bitcoin or monero preferred |
| **WISE (TransferWise)** | ✅ Moderate | ⚠️ Limited | Common alternative to crypto |
| **PayPal** | ❌ Low | ✅ Good | Many sellers don't accept (risk of account freeze) |
| **Bank transfer** | ❌ Low | ❌ Limited | Reveals banking details |
| **Western Union/MoneyGram** | ✅ Medium | ❌ None | Common scam payment method — be cautious |

## Shipping Considerations

| Factor | What to Know |
|--------|-------------|
| **Customs risk** | Packages may be opened by customs; estradiol is not a controlled substance in most countries |
| **Package size** | Small, discreet packages draw less attention |
| **Tracking** | Always use tracked shipping for accountability |
| **Delivery time** | International can take 2–8 weeks depending on origin |
| **Reshipment policy** | Reputable vendors offer reshipment if package is lost or seized |

## Discreet Packaging

Many vendors offer:

- Plain packaging (no medication labels on the outside)
- Labeled as "supplements," "vitamins," or "skincare products"
- Return address that doesn't reference medication

## What to Do If a Package Is Seized

1. **Do not sign for it** — if asked to sign, refuse
2. **Do not claim it** — ignoring customs letters is often safest
3. **Do not admit anything** — if questioned, say nothing and ask for a lawyer
4. **Contact the seller** — many will reship if notified
5. **Plan ahead** — always keep a backup supply

## Tips for Safe Ordering

- Use a separate email address for HRT orders
- Use a VPN when ordering
- Use cryptocurrency for payment privacy
- Keep your shipping address consistent
- Don't order large quantities at once (attracts attention)
