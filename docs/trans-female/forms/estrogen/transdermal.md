# Transdermal Estrogen (Patches & Gel)

## Overview

Transdermal estradiol delivers estrogen **through the skin** directly into the bloodstream, completely bypassing first-pass liver metabolism. This makes it the **safest route** for VTE risk and the route preferred by clinical guidelines.

## How to Apply

### Patches

1. Clean and dry the application site (lower abdomen, buttocks, or upper thigh)
2. Remove the protective liner
3. Apply firmly for 10 seconds
4. Rotate sites to prevent skin irritation
5. Replace according to schedule (weekly or twice weekly)

::: tip Patch placement tips
- Apply to clean, dry, hairless skin
- Avoid the waistline (belt/friction can loosen)
- Rotate between left and right sides
- Do not apply to the same spot twice in a row
- If it falls off, apply a new patch and note the day
:::

### Gel

1. Apply to clean, dry skin on shoulders, upper arms, or inner thighs
2. Spread a thin layer over a large area
3. Allow to dry for 3–5 minutes before dressing
4. Wash hands thoroughly after application
5. Do not wash the application area for at least 1 hour

::: danger Gel transfer warning
Estradiol gel can be transferred to others through skin contact. Once dry, cover the area with clothing. Avoid skin-to-skin contact with children, pregnant women, or partners at the application site until the gel is fully absorbed (30–60 minutes).
:::

## Dosing Guidelines

### Patches

| Target | Dose |
|--------|------|
| Starting | 50–100 mcg/24h |
| Typical | 100–200 mcg/24h |
| High | 200–300 mcg/24h |
| How to increase | Add more patches or switch to higher strength |

### Gel

| Level | Pumps (Oestrogel) | Sandrena |
|-------|--------------------|----------|
| Starting | 2–3 pumps/day | 1 mg/day |
| Typical | 4–6 pumps/day | 2 mg/day |
| High | 6–8 pumps/day | 3 mg/day |

## Comparison: Patches vs Gel

| Aspect | Patches | Gel |
|--------|---------|-----|
| Convenience | Apply twice weekly | Apply daily |
| Skin irritation | Common (rash, itching) | Rare |
| Level stability | Very steady | Steady with daily use |
| Dosing flexibility | Fixed strengths | Can fine-tune pump count |
| Transfer risk | Minimal (covered) | Higher (until dry) |
| Visibility | Visible on skin | Invisible when dry |
| Cost | Variable | Generally affordable |

## Pros & Cons

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Lowest VTE risk of all routes | Skin irritation (patches) |
| Steady hormone levels | Must apply consistently |
| No needles | Absorption varies between people |
| Bypasses liver metabolism | Patches can fall off |
| Preferred by guidelines | Gel requires daily routine |
| Good for older patients | Some find it inconvenient |

## Who Should Use Transdermal Estrogen

- People over 40 (lower VTE risk)
- Smokers (higher baseline VTE risk)
- Anyone with clotting risk factors
- Those who want the safest route long-term
- People who have had issues with other routes
