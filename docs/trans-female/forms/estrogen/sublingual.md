# Sublingual Estrogen

## Overview

Sublingual administration means placing an estradiol tablet **under your tongue** and allowing it to dissolve and absorb directly into the bloodstream through the oral mucosa. This bypasses first-pass liver metabolism, resulting in:

- **Higher bioavailability** — more estradiol reaches your bloodstream
- **Better E2:E1 ratio** — less conversion to weak estrone
- **Lower liver impact** — reduced clotting factor production
- **Faster peak levels** — but shorter half-life

## How to Take Estradiol Sublingually

1. Place the tablet under your tongue
2. Hold it there without swallowing
3. Let it dissolve completely (5–15 minutes)
4. Avoid swallowing saliva during this time
5. Do not eat or drink until dissolved

::: tip Not all tablets work sublingually
Generic estradiol hemihydrate tablets (like Estrofem) dissolve well sublingually. Some coated or branded tablets may not dissolve properly. Progynova (estradiol valerate) is designed for oral use and may not work as well sublingually.
:::

## Comparison: Oral vs Sublingual

| Aspect | Oral (Swallowed) | Sublingual |
|--------|------------------|------------|
| Bioavailability | ~5% (most lost to liver) | ~25% (direct absorption) |
| E1:E2 ratio | ~5:1 (high estrone) | ~1:1 (better) |
| VTE risk | Higher | Lower (not zero) |
| Half-life | 12–14 hours | 4–6 hours |
| Peak E2 | Low (30–60 pg/mL per 2 mg) | High (200–400 pg/mL per 2 mg) |
| Trough E2 | More stable | Lower trough; needs split dosing |
| Liver impact | Significant | Minimal |
| Convenience | Swallow and go | Must hold under tongue 5–15 min |

## Dosing

| Level | Dose | Frequency |
|-------|------|-----------|
| Starting | 1–2 mg | Every 8–12 hours (2–3× daily) |
| Typical | 2–3 mg | Every 8–12 hours |
| High | 3–4 mg | Every 8–12 hours |

::: warning Split your doses
Because sublingual estradiol has a shorter half-life (4–6 hours), you should split your dose into at least 2, preferably 3, daily doses. Taking it once daily results in very high peaks and very low troughs, which can cause mood swings and inadequate T suppression.
:::

## Pros & Cons

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Better E2:E1 ratio than oral | Requires multiple daily doses |
| Lower VTE risk than oral | Taste can be unpleasant |
| No needles | Inconvenient for some |
| Easy to adjust dosing | Not all tablets work sublingually |
| Inexpensive | Trough levels can be very low |

## Blood Test Timing

For sublingual estradiol, timing is critical:

- **Trough level** (most important): Test just before your next dose — this shows your minimum level
- **Target trough E2**: 100–200 pg/mL
- Do **not** test shortly after taking a sublingual dose (levels will be misleadingly high)
