# Expected Changes on Masculinising HRT

## Overview

Testosterone therapy induces male-puberty-like changes. The timing and extent of changes vary significantly based on age, genetics, dose, and overall health.

::: warning Individual variation is normal
Compare yourself to where **you** were last month, not to other people's timelines. If your levels are in target range, changes will come. Patience is key.
:::

## General Timeline

### Month 1–3 (Early Changes)

| Change | Details | Typical Onset |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| **Bottom growth** | Clitoral enlargement begins | 1–4 weeks |
| **Libido increase** | Sexual desire increases significantly | 1–4 weeks |
| **Body odour change** | Sweat smells more "musky" | 2–4 weeks |
| **Skin oiliness** | Face becomes oilier | 2–6 weeks |
| **Acne** | May begin, especially face and back | 3–8 weeks |
| **Menstrual changes** | Flow may lighten or become irregular | 4–8 weeks |
| **Voice beginning to change** | Slight drop, voice may crack | 4–12 weeks |
| **Appetite increase** | Hunger increases | 4–8 weeks |

### Month 3–6 (Noticeable Changes)

| Change | Details | Typical Onset |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| **Voice drop** | Significant lowering | 3–6 months |
| **Menstrual cessation** | Periods typically stop | 2–6 months |
| **Facial hair (peach fuzz)** | Fine hair on upper lip, chin | 3–6 months |
| **Body hair increase** | Legs, arms, abdomen, chest | 3–6 months |
| **Muscle mass increase** | Noticeable if exercising | 3–6 months |
| **Fat redistribution begins** | Fat shifts from hips to abdomen | 4–6 months |
| **Bottom growth continues** | Ongoing enlargement | 3–6 months |
| **Emotional changes** | Increased assertiveness, possible irritability | 3–6 months |

### Month 6–12 (Continuing Changes)

| Change | Details | Typical Onset |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| **Voice stabilises** | Voice reaches lower range | 6–12 months |
| **Facial hair thickens** | Coarser, darker terminal hair appears | 6–12 months |
| **Body hair coverage expands** | Chest, back, shoulders | 6–12 months |
| **Muscle definition** | More visible with exercise | 6–12 months |
| **Male pattern balding begins** | If genetically predisposed | 6–12 months |
| **Periods fully stopped** | Most trans men are amenorrheic by 6 months | 6–12 months |

### Year 1–3 (Peak Changes)

| Change | Peak |
|--------|------|
| Voice fully lowered | 1–2 years |
| Maximum facial hair | 2–5 years |
| Maximum body hair | 2–5 years |
| Maximum muscle mass | 2–3 years |
| Full fat redistribution | 2–5 years |
| Male pattern baldness | 2–5 years |

### Year 3–5+ (Late Stage)

- **Facial hair** continues to thicken and fill in
- **Body hair** may continue to increase
- **Scalp hair loss** (if predisposed) stabilises or continues
- **Body composition** fully masculinised

## Changes That Are Permanent vs Reversible

### Permanent (do not reverse if T is stopped)

| Change | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **Voice deepening** | Vocal cords permanently thickened |
| **Bottom growth** | Clitoral enlargement is permanent |
| **Facial/body hair** | Terminal hair remains but may thin if T stops |
| **Male pattern balding** | Hair loss is permanent |
| **Fertility changes** | May be permanent |

### Reversible (will revert if T is stopped)

| Change | Time to Revert |
|--------|----------------|
| Fat redistribution | Several months |
| Muscle mass | Several months |
| Menstruation | Usually returns within 3–6 months |
| Skin changes | 2–6 months |
| Acne | 2–6 months |
| Body odour | 4–8 weeks |
| Libido | 2–8 weeks |
| Emotional pattern | 4–8 weeks |

## Emotional and Psychological Changes

- **Increased confidence/assertiveness** — common and generally positive
- **Possible irritability** — especially if T levels are too high
- **Emotional numbing** — some report crying less easily
- **Improved mood / reduced dysphoria** — the primary benefit for most
- **Changes in attraction** — some report shifts in what they find attractive

## When to Worry

| Symptom | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Acne (mild-moderate) | Normal; treat with skincare |
| Severe cystic acne | See dermatologist |
| Pelvic pain/cramping (on T) | Check for ovarian cysts; see provider |
| Sudden severe headaches | Check blood pressure |
| Chest pain / shortness of breath | Seek emergency care |
| Severe depression / suicidal thoughts | Seek mental health support immediately |

## Tracking Your Changes

Consider tracking monthly:

- **Voice recordings** (say the same sentence each month)
- **Body measurements** (shoulders, chest, waist, hips)
- **Photos** — monthly in consistent lighting
- **Mood ratings** (1–10 scale)
- **Cycle tracking** (if periods continue)
