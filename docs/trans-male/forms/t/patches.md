# Testosterone Patches

## Overview

Testosterone patches deliver a steady dose of T through the skin over 24 hours.

| Brand | Strength | Frequency |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Androderm | 2–6 mg/day | Daily (apply each day) |

## Application

1. Apply to clean, dry skin on back, abdomen, upper arm, or thigh
2. Rotate application sites to avoid skin irritation
3. Press firmly for 10 seconds
4. Remove after 24 hours and apply new patch

## Pros & Cons

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Steady levels | Skin irritation common |
| Daily application | Adhesion issues (may fall off) |
| No needles | Visible patch |
