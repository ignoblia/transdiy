# Testosterone Gel

## Overview

Testosterone gel is applied daily to the skin, providing steady hormone levels without injections.

| Brand | Concentration | Typical Dose |
|-------|---------------|--------------|
| AndroGel | 1% (10 mg/g) | 25–100 mg/day |
| Testogel | 1% (10 mg/g) | 25–100 mg/day |
| Generic gel | Various | 25–100 mg/day |

## How to Apply

1. Apply to clean, dry skin on shoulders, upper arms, or abdomen
2. Spread a thin layer over a large area
3. Allow 3–5 minutes to dry
4. Wash hands thoroughly
5. Cover the area with clothing once dry

::: danger Transfer warning
Testosterone gel can transfer to others through skin contact. Cover the application area with clothing. Avoid contact with children, pregnant women, or partners until fully absorbed (1+ hours).
:::

## Pros & Cons

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Steady T levels | Daily application required |
| No needles | Transfer risk to others |
| Easy to adjust dose | More expensive than injections |
| Good for stable levels | Skin irritation possible |
