# Testosterone Gel

## Overview

## How to Apply

1. Apply to **clean, dry skin** on shoulders, upper arms, or abdomen
2. Spread a thin layer over a **large area** (not just a small spot)
3. Allow **3–5 minutes to dry completely** before dressing
4. **Wash hands thoroughly** with soap and water after application
5. Cover the area with clothing once dry

::: danger Transfer warning
Testosterone gel can transfer to others through skin contact. Cover the application area with clothing. Avoid contact with children, pregnant women, or partners until fully absorbed (1+ hours). If direct skin contact occurs, wash the area immediately with soap and water.
:::

## Pros & Cons

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Steady T levels (no peaks/troughs) | Daily application required |
| No needles (good for needle phobia) | Transfer risk to others |
| Easy to adjust dose (just change amount) | More expensive than injections per month |
| Good for sensitive skin (vs. patches) | Skin irritation possible at application site |
| Less drastic if supply chain breaks | Higher cumulative cost over time |
| Lower legal risk for import (gel < controlled than injectables) | Some find daily routine tedious |

### How to Measure Gel From a Tube

If you buy a multi-dose tube (like Testoboon 80g) instead of single-use sachets:

1. Know your target dose (e.g., 50 mg T/day)
2. For 1% gel: 50 mg T = 5 g of gel (about 1 teaspoon)
3. Use a **precision scale** (0.1g accuracy) to measure your dose
4. Apply the measured amount to your skin
5. Store the tube at room temperature, tightly capped

### How to Measure Gel From Sachets

Most sachets contain 5g of 1% gel = 50mg of testosterone. If your prescribed dose is different:
- **25 mg/day**: Use half a sachet (store the rest in a sealed container for next day — use within 24 hours)
- **50 mg/day**: Use one full sachet
- **75 mg/day**: Use 1.5 sachets
- **100 mg/day**: Use 2 sachets

## Avoiding Squeeze Tube Style Gels

::: warning
Do **NOT** buy squeeze tube style gels without clear dosage markings. These are typically designed for direct application to specific body parts (like the scrotum) and have **very different absorption rates** compared to standard shoulder/arm application.

The author has had good experience ordering from IndiaMart due to the lower cost and wider availability of options, but **only purchase sachet or clearly-dosed tube formats**.
:::

## Switching Between Gel and Injections

If you want to switch from gel to injections (or vice versa):

| Current Form | Switching To | Considerations |
|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Gel** | **Injections** | Start injections at standard dose; gel can be stopped immediately (no taper needed) |
| **Injections** | **Gel** | Start gel immediately after your last injection would have been due; it takes ~5 half-lives (3–4 weeks) for injected T to clear |

Always test blood levels 4–6 weeks after switching to confirm levels are in target range.
