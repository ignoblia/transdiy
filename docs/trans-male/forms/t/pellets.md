# Testosterone Pellets

## Overview

Testosterone pellets (Testopel) are small cylinders implanted subcutaneously, providing steady T release for 3–6 months.

| Brand | Dose | Duration |
|-------|------|----------|
| Testopel | 750–1,500 mg | 3–6 months |

## Procedure

- Inserted under local anaesthetic in a clinic
- Small incision in buttock or lower abdomen
- Pellets are placed subcutaneously
- Lasts 3–6 months before needing replacement

## Pros & Cons

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Set-and-forget for months | Requires medical procedure |
| Very stable levels | Difficult to adjust dose |
| No daily maintenance | Removal possible (rarely needed) |
