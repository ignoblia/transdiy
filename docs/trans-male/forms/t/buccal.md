# Buccal Testosterone

## Overview

Buccal testosterone (Striant) is a tablet that adheres to the gum, releasing T directly into the bloodstream through the oral mucosa.

| Brand | Dose | Frequency |
|-------|------|-----------|
| Striant | 30 mg | Twice daily (morning and evening) |

## How to Use

1. Place the tablet above your front teeth on the gum
2. Hold gently in place for 30 seconds
3. The tablet adheres and gradually dissolves over 12 hours
4. Replace with a new tablet after 12 hours

## Pros & Cons

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Steady levels | Gum irritation possible |
| No needles | Must check adhesion |
| Avoids first-pass liver metabolism | Tablet may fall off |
| | Less common/prescribed |
