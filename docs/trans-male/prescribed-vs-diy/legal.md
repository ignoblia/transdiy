# Legal Risks of DIY T by Country

## Key Difference from Estrogen

::: danger Testosterone is a controlled substance
Unlike estradiol, which is a non-controlled prescription medication in most countries, **testosterone** (and its esters) is classified as a controlled substance in the US, UK, EU, Canada, Australia, and many other jurisdictions. The legal risks for DIY testosterone are **significantly higher** than for DIY estrogen.
:::

## Country-by-Country

### United States

| Aspect | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Classification | **Schedule III controlled substance** |
| Possession w/o prescription | Federal crime — up to 10 years for possession with intent to distribute |
| Personal use | Technically illegal; enforcement varies |
| Importation | Illegal without DEA registration; customs seizures common |
| Practical risk | **Moderate to high** — risk of prosecution is real but low for small personal amounts |

### United Kingdom

| Aspect | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Classification | **Class C drug** |
| Possession | Illegal without prescription |
| Importation | Illegal; customs actively intercepts T shipments |
| Practical risk | **Moderate** — prosecution possible; packages frequently seized |

### Canada

| Aspect | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Classification | Schedule IV (controlled) |
| Importation | Requires prescription; seizures possible |
| Practical risk | **Moderate** |

### Australia

| Aspect | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Classification | Schedule 4 + anabolic steroid restrictions |
| Importation | Personal importation is illegal without prescription |
| Practical risk | **Moderate to high** — customs actively targets steroid imports |

### European Union

| Country | Risk Level | Notes |
|---------|------------|-------|
| Netherlands | **Moderate** | Controlled substance laws |
| Germany | **Moderate** | Prescription required; imports monitored |
| France | **Moderate** | Similar to Germany |
| Spain | **Lower** | Some T available with relatively low barriers |
| Czech Republic | **Lower** | Some T available OTC in pharmacies |

## General Recommendation

Because testosterone is a controlled substance in most countries, **DIY T carries significantly more legal risk than DIY estrogen**. If you can access prescribed T — even via private healthcare — it is strongly recommended.
