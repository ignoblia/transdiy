# DIY HRT (Trans Male)

## What Is DIY HRT?

DIY HRT means obtaining and administering testosterone without a prescription. This typically involves ordering from online pharmacies or homebrew suppliers, self-determining dosing, and self-monitoring via private blood tests.

## Why People Choose DIY T

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| Waiting lists | Prescribed care can take years |
| Gatekeeping | Some providers impose unnecessary barriers |
| Cost | DIY is often cheaper than private care |
| Control | Full control over regimen |

## Unique Risks of DIY Testosterone

::: danger Testosterone is a controlled substance
Unlike estradiol, testosterone is classified as a **controlled substance** in most countries:
- **US:** Schedule III controlled substance
- **UK:** Class C drug
- **EU:** Anabolic steroid control laws
- **Australia:** Schedule 4 (prescription-only) with steroid restrictions

This means importing, possessing, or selling T without a prescription carries real legal risk, including potential criminal charges.
:::

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Legal penalties for importation | Know your country's laws |
| Counterfeit/under-dosed products | Use community-verified sources |
| Contamination (homebrew) | Choose sources with sterile protocols |
| Incorrect dosing | Follow guidelines; test blood regularly |
| No monitoring for polycythemia | Get CBC tests every 3 months |

## Common DIY Testosterone Sources

| Source | Reliability | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Online pharmacies (require prescription) | High | Need a prescription |
| Grey-market T (no prescription) | Variable | Higher legal risk |
| Homebrew T | Variable | Community-produced; quality varies |
| Veterinary T | ❌ Do not use | Not sterile; not accurately dosed |

## Requirements for Safe DIY T

::: danger Minimum requirements
1. **Blood tests every 3 months** — especially CBC (hematocrit), total T, lipid panel
2. **Verified sources** — community-recommended vendors only
3. **Dose education** — understand starting doses, targets, and adjustments
4. **Emergency awareness** — know signs of polycythemia, DVT, PE
5. **Legal awareness** — understand the legal risks in your country
:::
