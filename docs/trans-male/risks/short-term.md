# Short-Term Side Effects

## Common Side Effects

### Acne

| Details | Management |
|---------|------------|
| Very common, especially face/back | Gentle cleansing twice daily |
| Peaks at 3–6 months | Benzoyl peroxide or salicylic acid |
| Usually improves after 1–2 years | For severe acne, see a dermatologist |

### Bottom Growth Sensitivity

| Details | Management |
|---------|------------|
| Clitoral enlargement begins within weeks | Normal; tenderness usually subsides |
| Can be tender during growth | Wear loose underwear if uncomfortable |

### Libido Increase

| Details | Management |
|---------|------------|
| Very common in first weeks/months | Normal expected effect |
| Can be intense | Usually stabilises over time |

### Hot Flashes

| Details | Management |
|---------|------------|
| Can occur as estrogen drops | Usually temporary (1–3 months) |
| Body adjusting to new hormone balance | Ensure T levels are not too low |

### Mood Changes

| Details | Management |
|---------|------------|
| Increased irritability possible | Monitor your mood |
| Some feel more confident/assertive | If severe, check T levels (may be too high) |

### Appetite and Weight Changes

| Details | Management |
|---------|------------|
| Appetite increases significantly | Expected — body needs fuel for muscle growth |
| Weight gain (mostly lean mass) | Healthy diet; regular exercise |

## When to Seek Medical Attention

::: danger Emergency signs
- **Sudden severe headache** — could be hypertension
- **Vision changes** — could be hypertension-related
- **Chest pain / shortness of breath** — could be PE or polycythemia-related
- **Severe depression / suicidal thoughts** — seek crisis support immediately
:::
