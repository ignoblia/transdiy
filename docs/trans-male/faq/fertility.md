# Fertility & Pregnancy

## How T Affects Fertility

Testosterone suppresses ovulation and menstruation, but the effect on fertility varies:

| Duration on T | Fertility Impact |
|---------------|------------------|
| 0–6 months | Gradually declining |
| 6–12 months | Significantly reduced |
| 1+ years | Usually low but not zero |

## T Is NOT Contraception

::: danger Pregnancy is possible on T
Documented cases exist of trans men becoming pregnant while on testosterone. Use contraception if pregnancy is a concern.
:::

## Fertility Preservation

| Option | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Egg freezing | Before starting T (ideal) |
| Embryo freezing | With partner or donor sperm |
| Ovarian tissue freezing | Experimental |

## Pausing T for Pregnancy

Some trans men pause T to become pregnant. T typically clears from the body within 2–4 weeks, and ovulation can resume within 3–6 months.
