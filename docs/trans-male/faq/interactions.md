# Interactions with Other Medications

## Testosterone Interactions

| Medication | Interaction | Action |
|------------|-------------|--------|
| **Anticoagulants** (warfarin) | T may increase anticoagulant effect | Monitor INR |
| **Insulin / diabetes meds** | T can improve insulin sensitivity | Monitor blood glucose |
| **Corticosteroids** | Additive fluid retention | Monitor |
| **Anticonvulsants** | Some may lower T levels | Monitor T levels |

## Interactions by Form

### Injections — No significant drug interactions beyond the above

### Gel — Transfer Risk

::: warning Testosterone gel can transfer
Gel can transfer to others through skin contact, including:
- Children (can cause premature puberty)
- Women (can cause masculinisation)

Cover application area with clothing. Wash hands thoroughly.
:::

## General Precautions

- Inform all doctors you are on T
- Check interactions when starting new medications
- Monitor blood work more frequently when adding interacting drugs
