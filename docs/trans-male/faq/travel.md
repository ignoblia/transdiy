# Traveling with T

## ⚠️ T Is a Controlled Substance

Unlike estradiol, testosterone is a controlled substance in most countries. Travel with T requires extra caution.

## Domestic Travel

- Keep in original labelled packaging
- Bring a copy of your prescription
- Declare injectables and needles at security
- Carry no more than a 90-day supply

## International Travel

| Situation | Likely Outcome |
|-----------|---------------|
| T with valid prescription | Usually fine |
| T without prescription | **Risky** — may be confiscated or questioned |
| T gel (larger bottles) | May be screened |
| Injectable T + needles | Likely inspected |

## Countries with Higher Scrutiny

- UAE — very strict medication laws
- Japan — strict importation rules
- Saudi Arabia — controlled substance restrictions
- Russia — anti-trans laws are a concern

## Tips

- **Always carry a prescription** if you have one
- **Keep a doctor's note** on letterhead
- **Check the embassy website** of your destination
- **Don't risk it** — if you're not sure about legality, find a way to get prescribed T
