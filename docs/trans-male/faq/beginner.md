# Common Beginner Questions

## How long until I see changes?

Bottom growth and libido increase can start within weeks. Voice changes and menstruation cessation typically take 2–6 months. Full masculinisation takes years.

## Do I need to take T forever?

Most trans men stay on T long-term. If you stop, some changes reverse (fat distribution, menstruation returns, muscle mass decreases) and some are permanent (voice, bottom growth, facial hair).

## What if I miss a dose?

- **Injections:** If a few days late, just take it. If more than a week, wait until your next scheduled dose.
- **Gel:** Apply when you remember. Don't double up.

## Will T make me bald?

Only if you have the genetic predisposition. Check your male relatives. Finasteride or minoxidil can help.

## When will my period stop?

Most trans men stop menstruating within 2–6 months. If bleeding persists beyond 6 months of adequate T levels, see a provider.

## Do I need top surgery?

Testosterone does not significantly reduce breast tissue. Top surgery is the only way to remove chest tissue. Some trans men are satisfied without surgery; others pursue it.

## Can I get pregnant on T?

**Yes.** T is not contraception. Pregnancy is possible. Use birth control if needed.
