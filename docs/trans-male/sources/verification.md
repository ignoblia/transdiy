# Verifying Product Quality

## Visual Inspection

### Vials
- Solution should be clear (colour varies by ester/oil)
- No cloudiness, particles, or crystals
- Crimp cap intact; no leaks
- Legible label with batch number

### Gel Packets
- Foil seals intact
- No leaks or discolouration
- Clear expiry date

## Blood Test Verification

The most reliable method: if your T level at a known dose is far below expected, the product may be under-dosed.

| Dose (injectable) | Expected Trough T |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| 50 mg/week | 300–600 ng/dL |
| 75 mg/week | 500–800 ng/dL |
| 100 mg/week | 700–1,000 ng/dL | Note: trough T > 1,000 ng/dL is above target and may warrant dose reduction |
