# Homebrew Testosterone

## What Is Homebrew T?

Homebrew testosterone is made by community members who purchase raw T powder and compound it into injectable solution with carrier oil.

## Risks

- **Legal:** Manufacturing T without a licence is illegal
- **Quality:** Variable concentration; no quality assurance
- **Sterility:** Risk of contamination if not properly prepared
- **Scams:** Risk of sending money and receiving nothing

## Precautions

- Only use community-verified brewers
- Check for visible contamination (cloudiness, particles)
- Compare blood levels to expected values
- Store properly (room temperature, away from light)

## Recommendation

Given the legal and safety risks, **prescribed testosterone is strongly preferred** over homebrew. If you must use homebrew, test your levels regularly and be aware of the legal consequences.
