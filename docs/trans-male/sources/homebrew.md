# Homebrew Testosterone

## What Is Homebrew T?

Homebrew testosterone is made by community members who purchase raw T powder and compound it into injectable solution with carrier oil. This is the most common way to access affordable injectable testosterone without a prescription.

### How It Works

1. **Raw testosterone powder** (enanthate, cypionate, or other esters) is purchased from chemical suppliers
2. The powder is **dissolved in carrier oil** (MCT, grapeseed, sesame, or cottonseed oil) at a target concentration
3. A **sterilising filter** (0.22μm) removes bacteria and particles
4. The solution is **filled into sterile multi-dose vials**
5. Vials are **labelled** with concentration, ester, batch number, and date

## Testosterone Ester Comparison

| Ester | Half-Life | Injection Frequency | Typical Conc. | Best For |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|---------------|----------|
| **Enanthate** | ~4.5 days | 2× per week | 200–300 mg/mL | TRT and cycles — most common |
| **Cypionate** | ~5 days | 2× per week | 200–250 mg/mL | US standard; nearly identical to enanthate |
| **Propionate** | ~2 days | Every other day | 100 mg/mL | Fast action; more PIP (post-injection pain) |
| **Undecanoate** | ~20 days | Every 4–6 weeks | 250 mg/mL | Longest ester; hard to source homebrew |
| **Sustanon (blend)** | Varies | 1–3 weeks | 250 mg/mL | Mix of 4 esters; rapid onset + sustained |

### Enanthate vs. Cypionate: Which Should You Choose?

For practical purposes, **they are interchangeable**. The difference is negligible:
- Enanthate is more common in Europe and from international sources
- Cypionate is the US pharmaceutical standard
- Same injection frequency, same dosing, same results
- Some users report very slightly less PIP with one vs. the other (individual)

## Risks of Homebrew

| Risk | Details | How to Mitigate |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| **Contamination** (bacteria, endotoxins) | Non-sterile brewing can introduce pathogens | Choose suppliers with sterile protocols; inspect vials before use |
| **Inaccurate concentration** | Powder may not be fully dissolved or incorrectly measured | Compare your blood test results to expected levels |
| **Allergic reaction** (to carrier oil) | Some oils (sesame, cottonseed) are common allergens | Know what oil is used; request MCT if you have allergies |
| **No pharmaceutical standard** | Homebrew is not USP/PhEur grade — no regulatory oversight | Accept the risk or use pharmaceutical sources |
| **Scams** | Never ships, incorrect product, wrong concentration | Use trusted, long-standing suppliers with community reviews |
| **Legal consequences** | Possession/import of controlled substance without prescription | Understand your local laws; consider prescribed options |
| **Abscess / infection** | Non-sterile injection or contaminated product | Use proper sterile technique; inspect solution before each use |

## Sterile Technique When Using Homebrew

### Drawing and Injecting

1. **Wash hands** thoroughly with soap and water
2. **Wipe vial septum** with 70% isopropyl alcohol — let dry (don't blow on it)
3. Use a **new, sterile needle and syringe** for each draw
4. Draw air equal to your dose, inject into vial, then draw medication
5. Change to a **new injection needle** before pinning (don't inject with the draw needle)
6. Clean injection site with alcohol in a circular motion, let dry
7. Inject at 90° angle (or 45° if subcutaneous), aspirate briefly, inject slowly
8. Dispose of sharps in a proper sharps container

### Vial Care

- Store at room temperature (15–25°C), away from direct light
- Do not refrigerate (can cause precipitation of the compound)
- Discard if you see **cloudiness, floating particles, or discolouration**
- Discard any vial that has been sitting unused for more than 12 months
- Use within **28 days of first puncture** (or as recommended by supplier)

## Recommendation

Given the legal and safety risks, **prescribed testosterone is strongly preferred** over homebrew. If you must use homebrew:

1. ✅ Choose a supplier with verified, recent community reviews
2. ✅ Request batch photos and HPLC test results
3. ✅ Start with a small test order before committing to a large purchase
4. ✅ Test your blood levels after 4–6 weeks to verify dosing
5. ✅ Use proper sterile technique for every injection
6. ✅ Be aware of the legal consequences in your country
7. ✅ Keep a backup supply (shipping delays happen)
