# Community Vendor Reviews

## Where to Find Reviews

| Platform | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Reddit (r/FTMDIY, r/TransDIY) | Largest review database |
| Discord servers | Real-time discussion |
| Telegram groups | Regional specific info |

## What to Check

- Recency (last 3–6 months)
- Blood test results from real users
- Multiple positive reviews over time
- Responsive customer service

## Writing a Review

Include:
- Product ordered and dose
- Price and shipping time
- Blood test results
- Overall rating
