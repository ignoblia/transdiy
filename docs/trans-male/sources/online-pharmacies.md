# Online Pharmacies

## ⚠️ Important Warning

Testosterone is a **controlled substance** in most countries. Importing T without a prescription carries real legal risk. This page is for educational purposes only.

## Types of Sources

| Type | Legal Risk | Reliability |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Licensed pharmacy (with Rx) | None | ✅ High |
| Online pharmacy (no Rx) | **Moderate-High** | ⚠️ Variable |
| Homebrew | **High** | ⚠️ Variable |
| Marketplace (eBay, etc.) | **High** | ❌ Low |

## Verification

- Check community reviews (r/FTMDIY, r/TransDIY)
- Ask for batch photos
- Test blood levels after starting

## Red Flags

- Prices too good to be true
- Only accepts untraceable payments
- No community reputation
- Refuses to provide product photos

## Gel Sources

Gels generally carry less risk of seizure, even if ordering from a non-domestic source.

::: warning
Do **NOT** buy squeeze tube style gels due to the difficulty of accurately measuring dose.

The author has had good experience ordering from IndiaMart due to the lower cost and wider availability of options.
:::

::: caution Pricing
Be very mindful of price, as many of the options below may be prohibitively expensive at the recommended dosages. Basically, avoid spending $100 on a box of gel packets if it will only last you two weeks.

If you are purchasing testosterone gel in packet form, confirm with the supplier whether the listed price is for a single packet or an entire box of packets, as pricing can vary significantly.
:::
