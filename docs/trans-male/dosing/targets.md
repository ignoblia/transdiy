# Target Ranges (Total T, Free T, E2, HCT)

## Primary Targets

| Marker | Target Range | Why |
|--------|-------------|-----|
| **Total T (trough)** | **300–1,000 ng/dL** | Male range |
| **Free T** | **50–200 pg/mL** | Active fraction |
| **E2** | **< 50 pg/mL** | Suppress feminising effects |
| **Hematocrit** | **< 54%** | Prevent polycythemia |

## What the Numbers Mean

### Total T

| Level | Interpretation |
|-------|---------------|
| < 300 ng/dL | Too low; increase dose |
| 300–1,000 ng/dL | ✅ Target |
| > 1,000 ng/dL (trough) | High; reduce dose |

### E2

| Level | Interpretation |
|-------|---------------|
| < 50 pg/mL | Well-suppressed |
| 50–100 pg/mL | Borderline |
| > 100 pg/mL | Elevated; consider aromatase inhibitor |

### Hematocrit

| HCT | Action |
|-----|--------|
| < 50% | Normal |
| 50–54% | Monitor; consider blood donation |
| > 54% | Reduce T dose or phlebotomy |
