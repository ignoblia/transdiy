# Adjusting Doses

## When to Adjust

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| T < 300 ng/dL (trough) | Increase dose |
| T 300–1,000 ng/dL | Maintain |
| T > 1,000 ng/dL (trough) | Consider reducing dose |
| HCT > 54% | Reduce dose or phlebotomy |
| Side effects intolerable | Reduce dose or change form |

## Adjustment Guidelines

| Current Dose | Adjustment |
|-------------|------------|
| T cypionate 50 mg/week | Increase to 60–75 mg/week |
| T cypionate 75 mg/week | Increase to 100 mg/week |
| T gel 25 mg/day | Increase to 50 mg/day |
| T gel 50 mg/day | Increase to 75–100 mg/day |

## Changing Injection Frequency

| Frequency | Pros | Cons |
|-----------|------|------|
| Weekly | More stable levels | More injections |
| Every 2 weeks | Fewer injections | Greater peak-to-trough swing |

## Special Considerations

- **Aromatisation:** High T may convert to E2; consider aromatase inhibitor if E2 > 100 pg/mL
- **SHBG:** Low SHBG means more free T at the same total T; may need lower dose
- **HCT:** If rising, reduce dose or donate blood
