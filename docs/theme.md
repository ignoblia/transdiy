# Theme Selection

Customize the color scheme of the wiki to match your preferences.

## Available Themes

The wiki uses the [Catppuccin](https://catppuccin.github.io/) color scheme by default. You can switch between different variants:

### 🎨 Catppuccin Mocha (Default)
- **Dark mode** with blue-based accents
- Easy on the eyes for nighttime reading
- Matches the original wiki design

### ☀️ Catppuccin Latte
- **Light mode** with soft pastel colors
- Better for bright environments or daytime use
- High contrast for accessibility

### 💜 Catppuccin Macchiato
- Dark mode with pink/red accents
- Vibrant and energetic color scheme

### 🌿 Catppuccin Frappe
- Dark mode with green-based accents
- Calming and natural color palette

## How to Change Theme

To switch themes:
1. Click the theme icon in the bottom-left corner of the screen
2. Select your preferred variant from the dropdown
3. Your choice will be saved automatically for future visits

## Custom Accent Colors

You can also customize individual accent colors:
- **Mauve** (default): `#cba6f7` - Used for highlights and links
- **Marble**: `#e6e3e6` - Neutral text color
- **Flamingo**: `#f2cdcd` - Soft pink accents
- **Pink**: `#f5c2e7` - Vibrant pink highlights
- **Red**: `#f38ba8` - Error/warning indicators
- **Maroon**: `#eba0ac` - Deep red accents
- **Peach**: `#fab387` - Warm orange highlights
- **Yellow**: `#f9e2af` - Warning/caution colors
- **Green**: `#a6e3a1` - Success/positive indicators
- **Teal**: `#94e2d5` - Info/neutral accents
- **Sky**: `#89dceb` - Light blue highlights
- **Sapphire**: `#74c7ec` - Blue-based accents
- **Blue**: `#89b4fa` - Primary link color
- **Lavender**: `#b4befe` - Soft purple highlights

## Theme Persistence

Your theme selection is saved in your browser's local storage, so it will remember your preference across visits and device sessions (if using the same browser).

## Accessibility Notes

All themes meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast ratios for text readability. If you have specific accessibility needs, please let us know in the community forums so we can improve the theme options.
