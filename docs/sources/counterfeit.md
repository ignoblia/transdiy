# Spotting Counterfeits

## Why Counterfeits Are Dangerous

- **Under-dosed** — no hormone effect (wasted money, delayed transition)
- **Over-dosed** — dangerous side effects
- **Wrong substance** — different medication than labelled
- **Contaminated** — bacteria, heavy metals, toxins

## Visual Inspection

### Pills
- Inconsistent shape, size, or colour
- Missing or illegible imprint codes
- Poor-quality blister packaging
- Expiry date missing or printed poorly

### Injectables
- Cloudy solution or visible particles
- Incorrect colour (varies by ester/oil, but should be consistent)
- Poor labelling (blurry, missing batch numbers)
- Crimp cap loose or damaged

## Blood Test Verification

The most reliable check: compare your levels to expected values for your dose.

## Red Flags

| Red Flag | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Price far below market | Likely under-dosed or counterfeit |
| No photos of actual product | Seller may not have it |
| Refuses batch info | Hiding something |
| Many negative reviews | Trust community consensus |
