# Spotting Counterfeits

## Why Counterfeits Are Dangerous

Counterfeit HRT medications pose serious risks:

- **Under-dosed** — no hormone effect (wasted money, delayed transition)
- **Over-dosed** — dangerous side effects (toxicity, blood clots, liver strain)
- **Wrong substance** — different medication than labelled (e.g., sugar pills, or worse — other active drugs)
- **Contaminated** — bacteria, heavy metals, toxins, fungal contamination
- **Non-sterile injectables** — risk of abscess, sepsis, or blood infection

## Injectable Medications (Vials)

### Visual Inspection Checklist

| Check | What Genuine Looks Like | ❌ Counterfeit Indicators |
|-------|------------------------|--------------------------|
| **Solution clarity** | Clear, no visible particles — colour varies by oil (MCT = clear, grapeseed = pale yellow, sesame = golden) | Cloudy, hazy, floating specks, fibres, crystals |
| **Vial seal** | Crimp cap is smooth, even, and intact | Loose, dented, or re-sealed cap; signs of tampering |
| **Rubber septum** | Clean, no visible wear before first use | Multiple puncture marks, discoloured, debris |
| **Label quality** | Professional printing, clear text, no smudging | Blurry text, misspellings, poor alignment, generic plain label |
| **Batch/lot number** | Clearly printed, alphanumeric, 6+ characters | Missing, handwritten, or appears on a sticker (not printed) |
| **Expiry date** | Present, legible, reasonable timeframe (2–3 years from mfg) | Missing, smudged, or > 3 years from manufacture date |
| **Concentration** | Clearly stated (e.g., "250 mg/mL") | Missing or ambiguous concentration |
| **Carrier oil** | Oil type listed | No oil specified |
| **Manufacturer** | Verifiable pharmaceutical company or known homebrewer | Unknown or fake manufacturer name |

### The "Float Test" for Oil-Based Injectables

Drop a tiny amount of the oil into room-temperature water:
- **Genuine**: Oil should bead up and float (lower density than water)
- **Suspicious**: If it immediately disperses or sinks, it may not be oil-based

⚠️ This is NOT a definitive test — use blood tests or HPLC for verification.

## Pills / Tablets

### Visual Inspection Checklist

| Check | What Genuine Looks Like | ❌ Counterfeit Indicators |
|-------|------------------------|--------------------------|
| **Shape and size** | Consistent across all pills | Varied sizes, irregular shapes, chipped edges |
| **Colour** | Uniform shade throughout | Uneven colour, speckled, spots |
| **Imprint code** | Clearly stamped, matches known reference | Missing, blurry, incorrect code, or easy to scrape off |
| **Score line** | Clean break line (if applicable) | Off-centre, barely visible |
| **Blister pack** | Professional foil, consistent pocket sizes | Cheap-looking foil, inconsistent pockets, easy to tear |
| **Packaging text** | Clear, no errors | Misspellings, grammatical errors, poor print quality |
| **Batch/lot number** | On both blister pack AND outer box | Missing from either; mismatched numbers |

### Common Imprint Codes for HRT Medications

| Medication | Common Imprint | Typical Appearance |
|------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Estradiol 2mg (Estrace) | "E 2" or "E2" | Blue, round, scored |
| Estradiol 2mg (Progynova) | "PG" or "2" | Blue-green, round |
| Spironolactone 25mg | "SP 25" or "25" | White, round, scored |
| Spironolactone 100mg | "SP 100" or "100" | White, round, scored |
| Cyproterone 50mg (Androcur) | "LA 50" or "50" | White, round, scored |
| Bicalutamide 50mg | "B 50" or "50" | White, round, film-coated |

## Gel Packets / Sachets

| Check | What Genuine Looks Like | ❌ Counterfeit Indicators |
|-------|------------------------|--------------------------|
| **Foil seal** | Intact, properly heat-sealed | Torn, re-glued, leaking, easy to open and reseal |
| **Gel consistency** | Clear to slightly opaque, uniform gel | Watery, separated, discoloured, grainy |
| **Labelling** | Professional, with brand name, strength, batch, expiry | Poor quality print, missing info |
| **Sachet material** | Thick, high-quality foil | Thin, cheap-feeling foil |
| **Manufacturer details** | Present and traceable | Missing or fake manufacturer |

## At-Home Testing Methods

### Water Test (Pills) — ⚠️ NOT Reliable

Some people recommend dropping pills in water or vinegar to observe behaviour (e.g., sinking vs. floating, colour change). These tests are **not scientifically validated** and should NOT be used to determine authenticity.

### Blood Test Verification — ✅ Most Reliable

The **single most reliable** way to verify your medication is to check your blood levels at a known dose:

| Medication | Expected Level at Standard Dose |
|------------|-------------------------------|
| Estradiol 4 mg/day (oral) | E2: 60–100 pg/mL (trough) |
| Estradiol 6 mg/day (oral) | E2: 100–200 pg/mL (trough) |
| Testosterone 100 mg/week (injectable) | Total T: 700–1,000 ng/dL (trough) |
| Spironolactone 100 mg/day | T levels should be suppressed (varies) |
| CPA 12.5 mg/day | T levels should be significantly suppressed |

If your levels are **far below** what the dose should produce, the medication may be under-dosed or counterfeit.

## Third-Party Laboratory Testing (Gold Standard)

For absolute certainty, send a sample to a testing lab:

| Test | What It Measures | Typical Cost |
|------|-----------------|-------------|
| **HPLC** | Exact concentration of active ingredient | $100–$200 |
| **GC-MS** | Full compound identification + quantification | $150–$300 |
| **Sterility testing** | Bacterial/endotoxin contamination | $50–$150 |
| **FTIR** | Quick identity check (is this the right substance?) | $50–$100 |

**Community testing pools**: Some Reddit communities organise group buys — multiple people contribute to test one batch, splitting the cost.

## Batch Numbers and Authentication

### What Legitimate Products Have

- **Clear batch/lot number** printed on the label
- **Format**: Alphanumeric, typically 6–12 characters (e.g., "B2401A", "LOT2401001")
- **Machine-printed** (not handwritten or stickered)
- **Cross-reference**: Some manufacturers have online verification portals

### Red Flags

| Red Flag | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Batch number is handwritten | Unprofessional, untraceable |
| Batch number on a sticker (not printed) | Easy to fake and replace |
| No batch number at all | No traceability |
| Multiple products share same batch number | Each product run should have a unique batch |
| Expiry > 3 years from manufacture | Unrealistic for most medications |

---

## Red Flags Summary

| ❌ Red Flag | Why It's a Problem |
|------------|-------------------|
| Price far below market average | Likely under-dosed or counterfeit |
| No photos of actual product (only stock images) | Seller may not have the product |
| Refuses to share batch info or testing | Hiding something |
| Packaging looks generic, no branding | Not from a legitimate manufacturer |
| Seller has many negative reviews in last 3 months | Trust community consensus |
| Pressures you to buy quickly | Prevents research; classic scam |
| Only accepts Western Union / MoneyGram | No buyer protection or recourse |
| Website looks unprofessional / full of errors | Likely a scam operation |
| Product looks different from community reference photos | Compare before using |
| Label has spelling mistakes (e.g., "Estradoil", "Testosteron") | Unprofessional; counterfeit indicator |

---

## What to Do If You Receive Suspect Medication

1. **Do not use it** — contaminated or incorrectly dosed medication can cause serious harm
2. **Take clear photos** — of the product, label, packaging, and batch number
3. **Post to community forums** — r/TransDIY, r/FTMDIY, or relevant Discord for verification
4. **Contact the seller** — some legitimate sellers will replace or refund
5. **Warn the community** — post a review to help others avoid the same issue
6. **If you already used it and feel unwell** — seek medical attention immediately
7. **Dispose safely** — do not flush; take to a pharmacy drug take-back program
